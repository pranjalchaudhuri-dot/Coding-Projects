Planet Earth — Quick Guide

5 Steps to Protect the Planet

  1. Switch off lights when leaving a room.
  2. Reduce, reuse, and recycle daily waste.
  3. Conserve water by turning off the tap while brushing teeth.
  4. Plant native trees and support local green spaces.
  5. Spread awareness about environmental conservation to friends and family.

Key Environmental Terms

Climate Change
A long-term shift in global or regional climate patterns, often refers specifically to the rise in global temperatures from the mid-20th century to present.
Renewable Energy
Energy derived from natural sources that are replenished at a higher rate than they are consumed, such as solar, wind, and hydro power.
Biodiversity
The variety of all living things and their interactions on Earth, including animals, plants, fungi, and microbial organisms.
