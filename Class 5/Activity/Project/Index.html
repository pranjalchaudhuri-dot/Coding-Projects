Healthy Eating Tips

Eating a balanced diet is essential for maintaining good health. Here are some tips to help you make healthier food choices:

  • Include a variety of fruits and vegetables in your meals.
  • Choose whole grains over refined grains.
  • Limit your intake of added sugars and saturated fats.
  • Stay hydrated by drinking plenty of water.
  • Practice portion control to avoid overeating.
